## Opening narrative *(draft)*

The pod does not land. It arrives.

There is the roar, and then the ground, and then a long quiet in which you understand that the ship is not coming back for you. Whatever CASPAR was, in the simulations, it is thinner here. The word it uses now is *arrival*.

The ground is real enough to hold a footprint, and something on it has noticed you. Bring whatever you learned — the sieve, the spell, the lab — or arrive with nothing and learn as you go. The world will not care which. It has been here a long time, and it has opinions about the people who come to stay.

Settle. Resist. Cross the threshold.
