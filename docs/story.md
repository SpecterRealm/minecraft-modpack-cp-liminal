## Opening narrative *(draft)*

This is not another classroom.

The ground is real enough to hold a footprint, and something on it has noticed you. CASPAR has stopped saying *scenario*. The word it uses now is *arrival*.

Bring whatever you learned — the sieve, the spell, the lab — or arrive with nothing and learn as you go. The world will not care which. It has been here a long time, and it has opinions about the people who come to stay.

Settle. Resist. Cross the threshold.
